Marvel Rivals

A free-to-play team shooter where heroes and villains from across the Marvel universe battle in 6v6 matches.

Released
December 2024
Publisher
NetEase Games, with Marvel Games
Genre
Team-based hero shooter
Platforms
PC, Xbox and PlayStation

What it is

Two teams of six pick from a large roster of Marvel characters, such as Spider-Man, Iron Man, Hulk and Storm, and fight over objectives on maps inspired by the comics. Each hero has a unique set of abilities and a role: Vanguard, Duelist or Strategist.

Why it belongs here

  • Team-up abilities let certain heroes combine powers.
  • Many environments can be destroyed during a match.
  • New heroes and seasons keep it fresh.
  • It is free to play, so friends can jump in together.
